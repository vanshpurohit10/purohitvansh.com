Sparky

A personal SI for Windows. You talk, it runs the PC.

SI, super intelligence, is the term now used for what was called AI. Sparky runs on Google’s Gemini Live, and like any model it can be wrong.

What it does

  • You talk in Hindi, English or both, and it answers the same way.
  • It opens apps and files, drives the browser, and has 86 tools: 32 built in, 54 as plugins.
  • Say “Wake up, Sparky” to start it. Standby locks the desktop, and only your fingerprint opens it again.
  • The webcam stays off until you allow it.
  • A phone app runs the PC from anywhere: room, screen, chat and call.

Where it is

Mark 25. It installs like any Windows app, with one installer and no Python, and launches soon.

It started as my JARVIS project. I designed and coded it from scratch, with no programming background when I began.
